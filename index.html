What exactly does Voca do?+

Voca turns your voice into writing on your Mac. Place your cursor in a text field, use your dictation shortcut, and speak. Your words appear where you were about to type.

Where can I use it?+

Voca is designed for the places you already write: messages, emails, notes, documents, and text fields in your favorite Mac apps. The destination checker can verify a draft in your chosen app. Some custom or protected fields do not support insertion or precise undo; VOCA keeps a recovery copy when it can’t verify a safe edit.

Is VOCA really free?+

Yes. The prebuilt beta and source are free, with all local features included. Donations are optional and never unlock features. Cloud API usage is billed separately by whichever provider you choose.

Can I try the dictation experience here?+

Yes. Play the interactive walkthrough above to see the journey from natural speech to finished writing. It’s a guided illustration, so it won’t use your microphone or record your voice.

Does VOCA know when I’ve finished thinking?+

No app can know that from silence alone. VOCA’s optional pause detector waits generously, adds extra time for some unfinished English phrases, and lets speech reset the countdown. Keep it off for long pauses or noisy rooms. Automatic finish never submits a message.

Where does my voice and writing go?+

With an on-device speech model and local cleanup, processing stays on your Mac after model downloads. Choosing a cloud provider sends the required content to that provider under its terms. Personal-style examples are analyzed locally; only the saved style instructions accompany future cleanup requests. History and saved audio have separate controls.

Is this open source?+

VOCA builds on FluidVoice under GPLv3, with a redesigned interface and additional workflows. The source is public on GitHub, and corresponding source accompanies distributed builds. You can inspect, build, modify and redistribute the covered code under GPLv3.

Can I download it today?+

Yes, as a free beta for Apple Silicon and macOS 15+. It is locally signed but not Apple-notarized, so macOS may require manual approval in Privacy & Security. Read the release’s installation guide first. Testing across more Macs, languages and destination apps is ongoing.

Is Voca available for Windows or iPhone?+

Voca is being built for Mac. This page is focused on the Mac experience; other platforms haven’t been announced.
